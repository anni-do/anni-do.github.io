## Projects 
#### [Legend of Zelda NES (Web Build)](https://anni-do.github.io/zelda-nes)

A Unity remaster of the Legend of Zelda on NES. Developed with Yeji Kim.

**Contributions:**
*  Player and Enemy movement, specifically: Stalfos, Gel, Keese, Goriya, Wallmaster, Aquamentus, and custom level enemies.

* Enemy AI (includes damage, collision behabior, stun and knockback)

* Enemy spawns and item drops

* Room transitioning & door mechanics (i.e. door unlocks and trap doors)

* Sprite animation & UI

* Debug and iteration; identified and fixed issues regarding collision behavior, movement, room transitions, and more.

* Custom level design!



**Tech**: Unity, C#, Aesprite, Piksel

### [Mixtures and Melodies (Web Build)](https://mi-miika.itch.io/mixtures-melodies)

A magic-themed rhythm game with rougelike/lite elements incorporated into its gameplay!
Players hit notes in time with the music, earn rewards based on their performance, and use modifiers that change gameplay between runs. 

**Contributions**

* Song chart mapping! All the songs available to play have custom made note charts.

* Core rhythm game mechanics and systems, including: input timing, feedback, and scoring

* Implemented a modification and currency system that follows along rougelike/lite games

* Gameplay UI & Artwork. Assets created by other artists are credited below

**Tech**: Unity, C#, Aesprite, Piskel
